# Epistemic Trace: Full History of Section 6

## Why Section 6 is the right illustrative example

Section 6 of the paper integrates Santoni de Sio & van den Hoven's meaningful human control framework into the central argument. §6.3 makes the methodological claim that documentation practice produces its own requirements — and that claim was empirically generated by the experience of writing Section 6 itself. The section is therefore both theoretically central and the maximally exercised case in the documentation system. Following its history from October 2025 to April 2026 lets a reader exercise the three Section 7 criteria (attribution, intellectual trajectory, understanding-and-endorsement) on a single concrete case, across two technological regimes and five distinct production stages.
