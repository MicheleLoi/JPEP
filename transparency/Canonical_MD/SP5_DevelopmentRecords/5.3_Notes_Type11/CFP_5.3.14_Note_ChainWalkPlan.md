# Chain Walk Plan

## What this is

Walk the v1/v2 input-output chain by reading the actual documents, not just their frontmatter. The session chain (CFP_5.3.5 Section A) tells us the structure; this walk verifies the links and discovers what the documents themselves reveal about the writing process.

## Why

SP-3 needs to narrate how the paper was written and argue that the documentation is adequate. That argument must be grounded in what the artifacts actually contain — not in metadata statistics. The chain walk produces the narrative backbone for SP-3.

## Method

Start from 4.1 (Complete Prompt, origin node). Read its content. Then follow its outputs forward through the chain, reading each document and noting:

1. What the document says about what it received and what it was asked to do
2. Whether the body content confirms, contradicts, or adds to the frontmatter links
3. Evidence of human orchestration: instructions, criteria, decisions
4. Evidence of endorsement: approvals, rejections, corrections

## Sequence

Follow the chain in CFP_5.3.5 Section A, session by session:

### Phase A: Origin + Section writing (Sessions #0–#8)

1. **4.1** (Complete Prompt) — the origin. What does it actually say? What framework does it set?
2. **4.7.1** (Epistemic Trace) — the other generic link. What context does it provide?
3. Walk sessions #1–#5 (Sections II–VI) via their guidance files (4.4.1, 4.4.2) and modlogs (4.2.2–4.2.7). Focus on: what did the human tell the AI to do? What criteria were set?
4. Sessions #6–#7 (planning: preliminary chat, PDL 7) — 4.4.4, 4.4.3. The planning layer before Sections VIII–IX.
5. Session #8 (Section VII writing) — 4.2.8. Inputs: 4.4.3 + 5.3.15. What did the synthesis note add?
6. Sessions #9–#10 (Section VIII writing, two phases) — 4.2.9. The richest modlog. Seven inputs in phase 1; manual insertion in phase 2.
7. Session #11 (Section IX writing) — 4.2.10. The cross-section input routing: why 4.3.1 and 4.5.1 alongside 4.4.6?

### Phase B: Audit + consolidation (Sessions #12–#14)

8. Session #12 (whole paper audit) — read 4.4.8, 4.4.9, 4.4.10. The three simultaneous guidance documents. What criteria did the human set?
9. Session #13 (consolidated Section 2 writing) — 4.2.5. How did the consolidation actually work?
10. Session #14 (post-editorial review) — 4.4.11. Trajectory claims check.

### Phase C: Appendix (Sessions #15–#16)

11. Session #15 (Appendix guidance) — 4.4.12. What did it receive and what did it specify?
12. Session #16 (epistemic trace sentences) — 4.4.13. The Nov 6 addition.
13. **4.2.11** (Appendix modlog, 7 sessions, 24 relational fields) — the exemplar. Walk its full structure.

## Output

After the walk: update CFP_5.3.13 (SP-3 writer briefing) with a new section summarising what the chain walk revealed. No separate epistemic trace needed — the findings go directly into the briefing that the SP-3 drafter will read.

## Scope note

This walk covers v1/v2 only (sessions #0–#16). Stage III and CFP-phase documentation are separate and better documented. SP-3's argument about v1/v2 is the harder case — if adequacy holds here, it holds everywhere.
