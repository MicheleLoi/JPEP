Epistemic responsibility: toward a community standard for human-AI collaborations


What I believe stands 

- need for a standard (an unstructured note is not enough)
- prominence as a principle
- replicability as a guiding principle (let's relate it to meaningful human control)


What we object to:
- standard can only emerge through protracted practice. It can't be an imposition from above
- the expectation can only develop gradually - as a community mediated process
- the idea of "demarcation of text" (inter textual clarity) relies on a primitive view of ai assistance. We could have papers that are almost entirely AI outputted. They could range from lots of human input (but full stylistic rewrite) to various forms of prompting to shaping workflows. We can't anticipate what the salient human input is
- yes that's what needs to be there in principle. in our paper SP1-3 are only review facilitators, but the real in principle replicability follows from the clarity of the process documentation
